Habitat Simulator Info

How to Play

Build your station by purchasing habitat, power, research, and storage modules. Your goal is to gather enough research before the mission timer ends while keeping the station powered and stable.

Mission Flow

  • Choose a difficulty.
  • Buy modules during the construction phase.
  • Press Start Mission to begin the mission.
  • Track power, research, and stored energy as time advances.
  • Complete the objective or survive long enough to score a good result.

Important Systems

Power production and consumption are tracked separately. If your station consumes more than it creates, stored energy drops. If your energy reserves hit zero, the mission can fail.

Research labs generate progress when power is available. The final mission score is based on research completion, power stability, and remaining budget.

Tips

  • Balance power generation and habitat needs.
  • Use labs early so research can accumulate.
  • Keep some stored energy ready for power dips.
